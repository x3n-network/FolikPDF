Folik 1.0.3 is our biggest update yet, with powerful new tools that remain private and on your device.

- Search your library faster with a private text index, matching-page counts, and highlighted excerpts.
- Protect Folik with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.
- Turn scans and PDFs into fillable copies. Folik can suggest likely fields on-device, and you can review, arrange, resize, or delete them.
- Inspect PDFs for metadata, forms, comments, links, attachments, scripts, and signature fields, then create a sanitized image-only copy.
- Run Sanitize PDF from Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight, or an automation.
- Create verified password-protected copies without storing your password.
- Compare the selectable text of two PDFs and review added, removed, or changed pages.
- Send PDFs to Folik directly from the system share sheet.
- Duplicate documents and restore or permanently delete several removed documents at once.
- Drag PDFs into the iPad library and open documents in separate Split View or Stage Manager windows.

Folik remains free and open source, with no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or document uploads.
